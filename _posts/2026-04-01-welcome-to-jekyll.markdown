The problem with difficulty balancing for Forza:
You have total control of the difficulty in every race, but also control of a heap of sliders which make the game harder and easier in a variety of ways. It is very hard to work out the ideal and optimal way to play for you. To make matters much worse, the difficulty between races sometimes seems very inconsistent. So one race on highly skilled can seem easy, while the next can seem extremely hard. There doesn’t seem to be many if any reliable indicators on whether it will be an easy or difficult race relative to the others. Car type, race type and even the specific track all seem to play some part. So it is near impossible to get a truly good, let alone ideal, difficulty curve. This makes the extrinsic satisfaction of being able to beat one race and move on to the next very obscured and unrewarding

The problem with progression:
Apart from the issues raised above with difficulty resulting in a totally flexible and hard to gauge progression through the championship races; there is also the issue of being given loads of money and cars early and easily and not really knowing what you’re working towards besides just more cars and upgrades purely for the intrinsic satisfaction of having and using them

These issues aren’t very important if you are motivated by getting better at the core mechanics and playing around with all the systems/tuning/car makes etc. But if you’re like me and like to have clear extrinsic feedback on how you’re getting better and what you’re accomplishing, it can make the games feel a little hollow and directionless
